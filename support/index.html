Common questions

Where are my readings stored?+

Your journal, notes, favorites, and personal meanings are stored locally on your device.

Can I read with physical cards?+

Yes. Choose Use My Own Cards to use Veil as a layout, card-entry, and meaning guide.

Can I disable reversed cards?+

Yes. Reversed cards can be enabled or disabled before a digital reading.

Which devices are supported?+

Veil 1.0 supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

How do I share a reading?+

Open a saved reading in your journal and use the Share button. Reading summaries can also be shared directly.
