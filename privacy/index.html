Privacy policy

Veil is designed to be a private, local-first tarot companion. Your reading questions, journal entries, notes, favorites, and personal card meanings are stored on your device.

Information we collect

Veil does not require an account and does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location, health data, or payment information.

Data stored on your device

Reading history and reader-created content remain in the app’s local storage. Deleting Veil may remove this information unless it is preserved by an operating-system backup.

Third-party services

Veil 1.0 includes no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Apple may process TestFlight and App Store information under Apple’s own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

Veil does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is a reflective reference tool and is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or financial guidance.

Changes to this policy

If Veil’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the corresponding app release.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@readwithveil.com. Beta testers can also use TestFlight’s Send Beta Feedback option.
