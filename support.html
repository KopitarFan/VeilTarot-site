Support

Veil is currently being tested through TestFlight. If you encounter a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Veil build number, and a short description of what happened.

Report a problem

Open Veil in the TestFlight app, choose Send Beta Feedback, and include a screenshot when it helps explain the problem.

Request a feature

Use Send Beta Feedback from Veil’s TestFlight page. Thoughtful reader feedback is shaping the first App Store release.

Common questions

Where are my readings stored?
Your journal and personal meanings are stored locally on your device.

Can I read with physical cards?
Yes. Choose Bring Your Own Deck to use Veil only as a layout and meaning guide.

Can I disable reversed cards?
Yes. Reversals can be enabled or disabled in your reading settings.

Which iOS versions are supported?
Veil 1.0 requires iOS 17 or later.
